The whole plan

How to work so you can concentrate

  • One question at a time. Don't scroll the long KMF page. Open a section in chapter 12 or the Exam Environment (chapter 11) and the screen shows one question only.
  • Answer first, then read. Read the logic route before the full working. The logic route is the part worth remembering.
  • Work in 25-minute blocks. One section, or six class problems, then a 5-minute break away from the screen.
  • Theory comes from the 10 pages. Use GregMat in 10 Pages for the ideas. Go to the full GregMat chapter only for a slide you need to see in full.
  • Write every miss down in one line. The idea, not the answer. The Error Log does the counting; your line does the learning.
  • Behind? Don't skip ahead. Do the oldest unticked tasks first. On a busy day, do the timed part and leave the review for the rest day.
